# References

Harvard (author-date) referencing. Each paraphrase, summary, quotation and borrowed idea gets an
in-text citation, `(Author, Year)` or `(Corporate Author, Year)`. Full details appear here in
alphabetical order, with the machine-readable version in [`references.bib`](references.bib).
Access dates are the real retrieval dates and are recorded when a source is first used. Corporate
authors, dates and titles are verified by hand.

## Chart footer convention

> Source: [Corporate Author] ([Year]); author's calculations. Data retrieved [date].

## Entry templates

**Corporate filing**

> [Company] ([Year]) *[Title of report]*. Form [10-K/10-Q]. Available at: [SEC filing URL]
> (Accessed: [date]).

**Online institutional research**

> [Institution] ([Year]) '[Title]'. Available at: [URL] (Accessed: [date]).

**Dataset or API**

> [Publisher] ([Year]) *[Dataset title]* [Dataset]. Available at: [URL] (Accessed: [date]).

**News article**

> [Publisher] ([Year]) '[Headline]', [day month]. Available at: [URL] (Accessed: [date]).
